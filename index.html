About The Author

Gursimrat Kaur

Gursimrat Kaur, born on July 24, 2009, in Chandigarh, is a dedicated student currently attending Shishu Niketan Public School, Sector 43, Chandigarh.

A passionate writer and avid reader, her writing reflects humility, calmness, joy and happiness. Through poetry, thoughts and articles, she aims to spread positivity and connect with people.

Inspired by Gurbaani, Punjabi literature and the beauty of Urdu, she continues to explore the world of words and express the emotions that live within.
